About me

Steph is a Game Development consultant who specialises in Indie Publishing, Production, Operations, Pitching, Business Development and Commercial Game Design. Having worked in the industry since 2013 on over 15 shipped titles, Steph has worked extensively across video games development, publishing and retail. She is also a highly respected advocate for diversity and inclusion within the industry.
